GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I am Mustapha Yahuza Kamfa, a Computer Science Education graduate of Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, with an NCE background in Biology and Computer Science. I am a passionate Python programmer and software developer with a strong interest in designing, developing and implementing technology-driven solutions. My technical experience includes Python programming, backend web development using Django and Flask, database management with PostgreSQL, and frontend development using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I enjoy turning ideas into functional applications and building practical software solutions that address real-world challenges. Through my development journey, I have worked on projects including school admission systems, school management applications and an inventory and sales management system. Beyond web development, I have a growing interest in software engineering, mobile application development, desktop software development, Artificial Intelligence (AI), machine learning and data analysis. I am committed to continuously expanding my technical knowledge, exploring emerging technologies and improving my problem-solving abilities. I also have an entrepreneurial mindset and an interest in business, innovation and commercial trading. I believe that combining technology, creativity and business knowledge creates opportunities to develop meaningful solutions and make a positive impact. My long-term vision is to become a highly skilled software developer and software engineer, capable of designing innovative applications, building intelligent systems and contributing to technological advancement. I am driven by curiosity, continuous learning, creativity and the desire to transform ideas into impactful digital solutions.

I enjoy learning by building projects, solving problems and understanding how applications work behind the scenes. My current learning journey includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Django and PostgreSQL. I believe in continuous improvement, clear communication and creating technology that is useful to people.
